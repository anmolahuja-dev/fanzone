# FANZONE - MASTER PRODUCT & DESIGN BRIEF

## Project Overview

Fanzone is a premium football-first social platform designed around clubs, matches, football events, and community.

The goal is NOT to build another Twitter clone.

The goal is to build the best place on the internet to be a football fan.

Users should be able to:

* Follow their favorite club
* Participate in matchday discussions
* Discuss football intelligently
* Track transfers
* Share memes
* Build football reputation
* Avoid the toxicity and engagement farming found on traditional social media

---

# Core Vision

Fanzone combines the best aspects of:

* Reddit communities
* Twitter discussions
* Sofascore matchday experience
* Discord communities

while removing:

* Rage bait
* Toxic fan wars
* Influencer-first experiences
* Algorithmic outrage

Football should always be the center of the experience.

# Visual Principles

## Minimalism

Every element must justify its existence.

If a component is unnecessary, remove it.

---

## Content First

Football content is more important than UI decoration.

---

## Premium Feel

The interface should feel:

* Calm
* Intentional
* Elegant
* Modern
* Timeless

---

## Whitespace

Use generous spacing.

Prefer too much whitespace over too little.

Screens should breathe.

---

# Typography

Typography is more important than colors.

Use a clear hierarchy.

Suggested scale:

Display: 32
Title: 24
Section Header: 20
Body: 16
Caption: 13
Metadata: 11

Avoid excessive bold text.

Use typography to create hierarchy.

---

# Colors

Use a restrained palette.

Avoid excessive colors.

Primary colors:

Light Mode:

* White backgrounds
* Neutral grays
* One accent color

Dark Mode:

* Near-black backgrounds
* Subtle surfaces
* Strong contrast

Only use club colors when relevant to club content.

---

# Cards

Cards should feel like Apple Music content cards.

Guidelines:

* Corner radius: 16-20
* Minimal shadows
* Soft borders
* Strong spacing

Do not use heavy shadows.

Do not use excessive gradients.

---

# Animations

Animations should feel premium.

Duration:

150ms - 300ms

Use:

* Ease in
* Ease out
* Ease in-out

Avoid:

* Bouncy animations
* Elastic effects
* TikTok-style movement

---

# Dark Mode

Dark mode is mandatory.

Design dark mode intentionally.

Do not simply invert colors.

Football fans will frequently use the app during night matches.

---

# Feed Design Rules

* Excessive badges

Focus on:

* Content

## Club Adaptive Experience

The application should dynamically adapt to the user's favorite club while maintaining a premium and consistent design system.

The core UI framework must remain identical across clubs.

Only accent colors, highlights, gradients, illustrations, and contextual animations should adapt to club identity.

The goal is to make users feel at home inside their football community without sacrificing elegance or usability.

The experience should feel similar to how Apple Music adapts to album artwork: personalized but controlled.

Themed elements include:

- Accent colors
- Header treatments
- Matchday experiences
- Notification highlights
- Profile identity
- Club hubs

Avoid excessive club branding.

Do not recolor entire screens.

Do not sacrifice readability.

Maintain premium aesthetics at all times.
