Fanzone — Product Vision
One Sentence

Fanzone is the home of football fandom — a football-first social platform where fans can discuss, celebrate, analyze, and experience football together without the toxicity and engagement farming of traditional social media.

What We Are

Fanzone is:

A football community platform
A club-centric social network
A matchday companion
A football discussion platform
A place to build football identity

Think:

Reddit + Sofascore + Twitter + Discord

But designed entirely around football.

What We Are Not

We are NOT:

Another Twitter clone
A generic social network
An influencer platform
A creator economy platform
A football news aggregator

Football comes first.

Personal brands come second.

The Product in One Paragraph

Fanzone is a football-first social platform built around clubs, matches, and football events rather than influencers and outrage. Users join their favorite club communities, participate in matchday experiences, discuss football, track transfers, share memes, and build reputation through meaningful contributions. The platform combines the community aspects of Reddit, the live engagement of Twitter, and the football focus of Sofascore while prioritizing healthy discussion, credibility, and fan enjoyment over engagement farming and toxicity.

This would be my "north star" document before writing a single line of code. Everything else—features, UI, database tables, AI capabilities, and system design—should support this vision.
